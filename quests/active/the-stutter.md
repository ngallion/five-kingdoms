# The Stutter

**Status:** Active — **Session 1 opens inside this quest**
**Type:** Main — the Forgeholm arc (Tier 1): the fair, the frame-up, and the race for the Deep Archive
**Given by:** The Mother Engine, mid-song
**Session introduced:** 001 *(planned)*

## Hook

Midsummer solstice. [The Wayfair](../../world/factions/the-wayfair.md) has raised its fair on the Cinderfield outside [Kaldrum](../../locations/kaldrum.md) — its regular turn on the circuit, familiar and beloved — and the party, drawn from different kingdoms, is on the fairground the night the Loosening peaks. Two oddities for observant players: the Myriad's stage is dark ("resting his voice"), and [Wrena Calder](../../characters/npcs/wrena-calder.md) speaks a consecration over a mended valve and goes very still when it *takes*.

Then, while the fair is singing, the city's one constant changes: **the Mother Engine stutters.** Output drops across all of Kaldrum; forty thousand people wake like a sleeper whose heart skipped. The Guild wants a culprit. [The king's Provisioner](../../characters/npcs/ilsabet-krane.md) has one ready — and "protection" besides.

**On-ramps — pick one or stack several** *(deliberately left open; choose at session zero once you know the party)*:

1. **The frame nets them too.** Outlanders from four kingdoms, seen fraternizing at the fair the night the Engine skipped — the culprit-story writes them in as the fair's foreign agents. Gates close; marks are checked; clearing the fair clears themselves. *(Given Krane's posture below, this happens by default unless you soften it.)*
2. **Sera asks them.** The Fairkeeper reads the party — five strangers who braid well — and quietly asks for the help the fair can't request officially.
3. **They saw something no one else saw.** At the skip's moment, mid-Loosening, the party perceived something real — the hum's *shape*, a direction, a pull toward the deep city. They hold a clue that exists nowhere else, and both Vorn and Krane will eventually want it.
4. **A taking, night one.** The party befriends a rigger at the fair; by morning she's gone — one of the [Quiet Harvest's first Kaldrum victims](the-quiet-harvest.md). The investigation starts personal, and the "fair-folk fleeing in the night" feed the frame-up.

## Objective

*(As the party understands it)* Prove the fair didn't do it — before the Bench of Masters rules that it did — and find out what actually made the Engine skip.

## What's Really Going On

Three machines are running at once, and only one of them is visible:

- **The stutter is the lattice, not the fair.** The fair has camped here dozens of times and the engines barely flickered; what changed is the strain of [Korrven's re-tuning of Agoran](../../world/factions/the-wayfair.md#gm-notes) since 209, which the Mother Engine — the master tap — carries all of. This solstice, the usual push met a newly cracked wall.
- **The frame-up predates the fair.** Krane's instructions were pre-written: blame the fair, offer protection, make Forgeholm ask *him* for help ([her file](../../characters/npcs/ilsabet-krane.md#secrets)). The [Agoran Compact](../../characters/npcs/ilsabet-krane.md#secrets) is the real weapon — clause by renewing clause, certification moves under the king's provisioning authority, ending with his inspectors at the Root.
- **The archive race is already running.** Krane's clerks are asking about "precedents from the nineties." Her standing order: bring the king the sealed record of the Tamperer's Tribunal, unread. The party doesn't know the record exists yet. They will.

Meanwhile, at the arc's edges: two riggers and a lamp-mender vanish from the fair ([the Quiet Harvest](the-quiet-harvest.md)), and a Girdle waystation is saved from driven march-things by Wardens who arrive suspiciously fast ([the Hollowing](../../world/the-hollowing.md)).

## Clues & Evidence

*The true trail:*
- **Old-timers' memory / Ondrel's arithmetic:** every prior fair made the engines flicker *faintly* — the difference isn't the fair, it's the wall ([why this fair, why now](../../world/factions/the-wayfair.md#gm-notes))
- **Vorn's own files:** the Engine's draw-figures haven't summed for forty years, filed as *instrument error* — and the discrepancy jumped in 209, before this fair ever arrived ([her secrets](../../characters/npcs/halda-vorn.md#secrets))
- **The braid breadcrumb:** magic braided near the Root makes the hum skip — a breadcrumb or an accusation, depending on who's watching ([Kaldrum](../../locations/kaldrum.md#gm-notes))

*The manufactured trail (the frame):*
- **The saboteur kit:** charge-sets and cut cable in the vent-galleries, in crates sealed — habit of a woman who seals everything — with the **Agoran grain-sheaf**
- **The coached witness:** a Chimneys informer whose testimony is *too* rehearsed, and whose family's debts were recently, quietly bought
- **The written-in party:** correspondence in outland hands, each PC's assigned motive eerily specific *(see GM Notes — Krane's play)*
- **The "fled" fair-folk:** three empty bunks the morning after the skip, presented as flight — actually the Harvest's first Kaldrum takings

*The threads out:*
- **Krane's clerks and the nineties** — the party's first pointer toward the Deep Archive
- **The waystation's dark beacon** — doused the evening *before* the attack ([the Dousers](../../world/the-hollowing.md#gm-notes))

## GM Notes

**Acts and clocks.** Act 1 is Session 1: the fair at full Loosening, the party meeting, the skip — end the session on the morning after, with the culprit-story already in every mouth. *(Run the fair night itself from [the Fairground kit](../../world/factions/the-fairground.md): games, performers, food, and the night's five-beat clock — the skip lands mid-act 4, at full song.)* Act 2 (roughly two in-world weeks): the fair confined, the gates marks-checked, the investigation web open — run the saboteur catch, the waystation rescue, the Chimneys door, and the Ondrel hunt in whatever order the party pulls threads. Act 3: the tribunal, date set by the Bench at Act 2's midpoint so the players feel the clock. Background clocks, advancing when the party stalls: **the tribunal date**, **the Compact vote** (clauses ratified one Bench session at a time), **Ondrel's warrant**, and — the long fail-clock — **inspectors at the Root by season's end**.

**The three levers converge at the tribunal.** Any one can carry the verdict; all three together break the Compact too:

1. **The defense.** Outlanders have no standing before the Bench — someone must sponsor them into the hall: Corwith quietly, Vorn's fairness formally (she will honor procedure even for people she distrusts), or Wrena packing the gallery with every Chimneys family the fair ever mended something for. The scene to play: someone argues at the anvil where *nobody argued in 195* — and Vorn, presiding, hears the echo whether she wants to or not.
2. **The measurement.** Vorn follows numbers anywhere. The winning number: *the draw changed in 209, citywide, before this fair ever arrived* — assembled from Ondrel's arithmetic plus Vorn's own filed "instrument error." It clears the fair without revealing the anchors (Ondrel will not say what he actually suspects in open hall — yet). Getting Ondrel there alive and un-arrested is the adventure.
3. **The archive race.** Pell Lunt cannot be bribed but can be convinced the seal is being abused — and he has noticed Krane's clerks. Staging option per canon: the record *leaves* the archive mid-arc (theft, Vorn moving it, or fire) and becomes portable. Whoever holds it at arc's end holds the campaign's best reveal document.

**The reveal ladder** *(decided)*: if the party wins the record, they learn **the name** — the condemned wright Herrek, the line scoured, the son's name in the ledger: *Korrven*. Kaldrum-born, Guild-made, king at the gates. That is the arc's thunderclap, and it is enough. The deeper layers stay gated: the splice diagrams read as a dead graft to anyone but a trained enginewright of that generation — **Ondrel** could read them, if he trusts the party, if he's safe, and his reading ("the splice *took*") belongs to a later beat, GM's timing. Herrek's sealed effects — the Architect's notes — stay unread regardless; nobody alive can parse them yet.

**Krane's play** *(decided — she frames, not courts)*: the party gets no seduction; they get machinery. She reads each PC's lack the way her king taught her — and writes it into the evidence *as their motive*: the markless PC "came for marks," the exile "came for revenge on guilds," the drifter "was bought." Played right, each PC should feel briefly, coldly *seen* by their own frame — the first taste of what the king does, delivered as prosecution. Repurpose her stat move **The Offer** accordingly: same mechanic, but what she names aloud is the motive she's assigned them. She remains courteous throughout and legally untouchable the first two times they corner her.

**Encounters** *(decided — saboteurs + the driven tide; sketch stats, full adversary files later)*:
- **The false-flag crew** *(Tier 1)* — Compact linemen staging "fair sabotage" in the vent-galleries: 3–5 skirmishers (Difficulty 11, mob tactics, flee when the kit is compromised) plus one **quiet-corps handler** (Difficulty 13, fights to *burn the evidence*, not to win). Catching them mid-plant is the arc's best single scene: the fight *is* the clue, and the grain-sheaf seals survive if the party protects the crates.
- **The waystation drive** *(Tier 1)* — a Girdle waystation hit by driven **Strays** and a **Chorus** pack ([sketches](../../world/the-hollowing.md#the-march-things); Difficulty 10–12, they spotlight whoever has no ally in Close range). Mid-fight, the King's Wardens arrive — competent, heroic, *pre-staged* — and the rescue does more for the Compact's votes than any speech. A party that checks the beacon-line finds it was doused the night before.
- **Optional dread:** a Missing Man on the Made Range at dusk (no stats needed — it never closes distance; it just has someone's walk), and the Gleaner working the Grey After, offering a wounded NPC exactly what they lack.

**Outcome ladder.** **Full win:** the fair cleared in open hall, the Compact's certification clauses struck or the vote collapsed, the record in the party's custody — and Krane withdraws in good order, already drafting the next approach; the king's timetable slips a season. **Partial:** the fair cleared but the Compact stands — Tier 2 begins with the door still opening, clause by clause. **Loss:** the fair convicted and banished (or held), the protection accepted — run [the contingency](../../locations/kaldrum.md#gm-notes): Kaldrum falls *by invitation*, the party likely outlawed, Wrena stays, and the campaign continues as liberation. Losing here should hurt, not end the game.

**Threads this arc must seed** (whatever the outcome): the empty bunks and the quiet wagons ([the Quiet Harvest](the-quiet-harvest.md)); the doused beacon ([the Hollowing](../../world/the-hollowing.md)); Wrena's consecration and the braid-stutter breadcrumb ([the magic reveal chain](../../world/magic.md#gm-notes)); Tam's dark stage and the fair's next stop — **midwinter at the Stoa** ([the Sophara arc](../../world/kingdoms/sophara.md#gm-notes)); and Vorn's two rattles — the splice she measured and the blank stamp she's never questioned ([her file](../../characters/npcs/halda-vorn.md#gm-notes)). If the party leaves Forgeholm holding the record, Ondrel's trust, and the Stamp's friendship, the campaign is fully armed.
